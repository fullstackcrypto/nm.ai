1 — Adjustable operator platform

Current issue

Some refrigeration assembly stations sit too low for taller assemblers, causing repeated bending, poor posture, and extra strain during the shift.

Proposed improvement

Add an adjustable operator platform at selected stations so the working height can better match the assembler.

Main benefit

A simple ergonomic improvement that can make the work smoother, safer, and more consistent without changing the full station layout.
